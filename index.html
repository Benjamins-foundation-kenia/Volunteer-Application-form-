About Us

Our Day Care Center in Mombasa

We are a registered non-profit Day Care Center located in the vibrant coastal city of Mombasa, Kenya. Currently, we look after more than 30 beautiful vulnerable children ranging from 3 to 12 years old, offering them free early-childhood learning, safety, and pathways out of poverty.

An Authentic Cultural Experience: As a volunteer, you will get to stay directly with the loving family of our school director! Enjoy the incredible coastal weather, bond over homecooked local dishes, and immerse yourself fully in community life. Send us a message to find out more!

Airport Connections

We coordinate smooth arrival pathways via Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) or directly through Moi International Airport (MBA) right here in Mombasa.

Who Can Join?

Independent travelers must be 18+, but we also extend warm invitations to complete families traveling with children of their own!

Financial Integrity Note

Zero Program Fees

As a grassroots community mission, we do not charge anything to volunteer with us. To sustain our daily kitchen and vital operations seamlessly, volunteers simply cover their direct living utilities.

All-Inclusive Living Fee
€12 / Day
Covers: 3 healthy meals a day, private bedroom space, household WiFi access, safe clean drinking water, toilet paper, and kitchen cooking gas.

What You Get In Return

  • Private bedroom in a safe host family home environment
  • Discounted and 100% free trips across Kenya visiting National Parks & Safaris
  • Organized campings and exploration trips during weekends
  • Beautiful authentic African gifts to take back home to your family
